# Review Rubric

Review in this order.

## 1. Product fit

- Does the interface match the product archetype and user task?
- Is the expression level appropriate?
- Did the design preserve necessary information density?

## 2. Hierarchy

- Can the primary action or information be found immediately?
- Are size, containment, and type doing the hierarchy work before color?
- Are secondary actions visibly quieter?

## 3. Material 3 system

- Are colors semantic roles rather than random raw values?
- Are surfaces layered tonally?
- Is shape varied by role rather than one universal radius?
- Are components recognizable and predictable?

## 4. Google-family character

- Does the product feel soft, geometric, clear, and optimistic without looking childish?
- Are the four brand anchors selective rather than scattered?
- Are icons simple and legible?
- Do expressive moments use color/shape/motion coherently?

## 5. Motion

- Does motion communicate cause, continuity, state, or hierarchy?
- Are recurring interactions restrained?
- Are hero interactions allowed more expressive springs/morphing?
- Is reduced motion supported?

## 6. Accessibility

- Text contrast meets WCAG AA for the actual size/weight.
- Focus is visible.
- Touch targets are usable.
- Color is not the only status signal.
- Labels and errors are understandable.

## 7. Responsive behavior

- Does the interface recompose rather than merely shrink?
- Are navigation and panes appropriate for the viewport?
- Is dense content still usable on smaller screens?

## Failure patterns to call out immediately

- "Google style" implemented as four colors on generic components.
- Generic SaaS dashboard with identical 12-16 px rounded cards everywhere.
- Black/neon crypto look despite a Material 3 brief.
- All four brand colors used as equal navigation/status colors.
- Giant pill buttons everywhere.
- Card soup.
- Heavy shadows or glassmorphism replacing tonal surfaces.
- Expressive animation on every hover.
- Meaningless gradient decoration.
- Red/green brand accents conflicting with finance semantics.
- Exact copying of Google logos, icons, or recognizable product layouts.

## Finding format

For each meaningful issue, report:

- **Element** — exact location/component.
- **Problem** — concrete failure.
- **Why** — hierarchy, semantics, Material rule, accessibility, density, or consistency.
- **Fix** — exact role/component/shape/motion change.
