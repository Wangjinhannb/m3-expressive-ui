## Cultural references

Any museum, university, archive or Sketchfab imagery/model used during research must be recorded with:
- source URL;
- creator/institution;
- license;
- whether the asset is research-only or shipped in the final build.

Do not ship an asset merely because it is visible online.

## Generated reference imagery

Generated concept frames may be used only as lighting/composition targets. They must not be presented as historical objects, museum records or exact reconstructions of a traditional Foshan lion head.
